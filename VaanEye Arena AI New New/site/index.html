About Us
எங்களைப் பற்றி

Space data belongs to everyone

Billions of rupees of satellite infrastructure already orbit above India. The data is free. The problem is nobody can read it.

The problem. A farmer opens one app for weather, another for crop advice. A fisherman uses a third for fishing zones and still crosses the maritime border by accident. Raw NDVI rasters and SAR scenes mean nothing to them.

Our answer. VaanEye is a single window over Land, Water, Sky and Human Safety. We ingest open data from ISRO Bhuvan, NASA Landsat and ESA Copernicus, run the geospatial maths on our servers, and deliver one plain sentence: "Fire 12 km from your village — move north."

Nobody left behind. Smartphone users get an interactive map. Everyone else gets an SMS or an automated voice call in Tamil. That hybrid GSM fallback is why VaanEye works in villages where other apps simply do not load.

How It Works
எப்படி வேலை செய்கிறது

From orbit to your pocket in four steps

1
தரவு சேகரிப்பு

Ingest

Python workers pull fresh .TIFF bands from ISRO Bhuvan, Copernicus and USGS the moment a satellite passes overhead, then strip cloud noise.

2
பகுப்பாய்வு

Analyse

GDAL and NumPy convert pixels into NDVI, NDWI and LST matrices. PyTorch CNNs scan SWIR bands for thermal anomalies and diff yesterday's scene.

3
பொருத்துதல்

Match

PostGIS R-Tree indexes intersect every result against your saved land polygon, sea zone or home pin in milliseconds.

4
அறிவிப்பு

Alert

Smartphones get a realtime push. Feature phones get an SMS and an automated IVR voice call in Tamil through a GSM gateway.
